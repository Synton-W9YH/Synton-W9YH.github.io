## Overview

The W9YH Repeater is our local repeater hosted east of the Siebel Center for Computer Science, shared with the WILL broadcast station tower. All ham radio operators are invited to talk, as well as invited to our weekly nets currently on Wednesday nights at 9 PM Local Time.
## Key facts

| Item             | Value       |
| ---------------- | ----------- |
| Output Frequency | 444.525 MHz |
| Input Offset     | +5.000 MHz  |
| PL Tone          | 162.2 Hz    |
